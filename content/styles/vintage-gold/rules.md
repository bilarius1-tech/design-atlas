# Rules · vintage-gold

## Do

1. Warm aged-paper ground `#E9DDC6`, ink `#352A20`.
2. Gold `#B58A45` for primary; bronze/olive sparingly in frames and dividers.
3. Playfair Display for headings; Manrope for body with Cyrillic.
4. Thin gold lines / inset frames — not glitter fills.
5. Heritage atmosphere without invented history.

## Don’t

1. Don’t invent awards, dates, stamps, seals, or logos.
2. Don’t distress or age product materials in prompts.
3. Don’t flood gold across every surface.
4. Don’t drift into neon / streetwear / dense-tile.
5. Don’t invent a second palette outside `tokens.json`.

## Composition

- Default: `editorial-split`
- Structure from user chat; Atlas = visual system only
- Preview may add paper wash + thin gold inset frame via scoped CSS

## Accessibility

- Ink on paper ≥ AA
- Gold CTAs: dark label `#352A20`
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`
