# Research notes · warm-premium

## Intent

Warm dark premium system for DESIGN ATLAS — queue #3. Owned SSOT: InfoStyle `premium` (тёплый премиум). Adapted for **web landing / app shell**, not marketplace image-gen product cards.

## Traits

- Deep warm ground `#26211F`, cream ink `#F3EDE2`
- Signals: gold `#C7A66A`, blush `#D6B7B0`, sage `#8DAF9D`
- Soft radial lift / material calm — restrained luxury
- Type: **Playfair Display** headings; Manrope / DM Sans body
- Distinct from neon glow and streetwear campaign loudness

## Anti-patterns

- Loud neon / streetwear poster blocks
- Soft clay pastel toys
- Fake logos, awards, “luxury” clutter
- Cold clinical blue-gray as the hero (that’s later `clinical-minimal`)
