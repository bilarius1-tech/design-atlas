# Research notes · vintage-gold

## Intent

Premium vintage / warm gold paper system for DESIGN ATLAS — queue #7. Owned SSOT: InfoStyle `vintage`. Adapted for **web landing / app shell**. Heritage atmosphere only — no invented awards, dates, seals, or logos.

## Traits

- Warm aged-paper ground `#E9DDC6`, ink `#352A20`
- Signals: gold `#B58A45`, bronze `#8A6B4B`, olive `#6F7A58`
- Thin gold frames / hairline dividers; restrained editorial type
- Type: **Playfair Display** / Cormorant Garamond; Manrope body
- Distinct from warm-premium (dark stage) and eco-botanical (leaf craft)

## Anti-patterns

- Invented awards, stamps with fake years, heraldic logos
- Neon / streetwear / dense industrial mosaics
- Aging/distressing product materials in prompts
- Gold flood across every surface
