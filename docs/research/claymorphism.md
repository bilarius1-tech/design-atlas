# Research notes · claymorphism

## Intent

Soft “clay / plasticine” volume for DESIGN ATLAS — first complex soft-volume proof after Swiss / Midnight / Poster. Original Atlas pack; owned catalog [dizain-prompty claymorphism](https://ztim2008.github.io/dizain-prompty/stil/claymorphism/) is the SSOT for palette and rules. designprompts.dev is loop reference only (not a clone).

## Traits (facts)

- Cool pastel ground `#F1F2FA`; cream surface `#FFFDF7`; plump cards; radii ~28–38px
- Dual soft shadow: outer tinted bottom-right + light inner top-left
- Multi-pastel planes: pink / mint / sky / warm cream (kid clay set, not single violet wash)
- Friendly family/kids tone; Cyrillic-safe Nunito / Manrope / Montserrat Alternates

## 1.1.0 note

Aligned toward multi-pastel “детский” clay volume after visual-direction review of designprompts.dev/claymorphism (reference only — no clone of DP fonts/copy/graphics).

## Anti-patterns

- Flat material cards with hairline borders only
- Hard neo-brutal offsets
- Glass blur as primary identity
- Tiny 4–6px radius systems
