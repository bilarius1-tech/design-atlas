# Research notes · industrial-power

## Intent

Brutal industrial / signal-yellow system for DESIGN ATLAS — queue #8. Owned SSOT: InfoStyle `industrial`. Adapted for **web landing / app shell**. Pairs with **dense-tile** + `spacing.gap: 0px` (Taptop / kmprom-like flush mosaics).

## Traits

- Dark graphite ground `#252522`, cream ink `#F4F1E9`
- Signal accents: yellow `#FFC400`, orange `#FF6A00`, red `#E55353`
- Hard edges, flush feature/stat tiles, directional light feel
- Type: **Oswald** / Unbounded display energy; Roboto body
- Distinct from `blueprint-grid` (cyan engineering) and `poster-brutal` (light paper constructivism)

## Anti-patterns

- Soft clay / neu / frosted glass
- Warm editorial paper luxury
- Invented safety certifications or fake industrial seals
- Neon acid bloom as the main idea
