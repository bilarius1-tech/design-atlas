# Research notes · clinical-minimal

## Intent

Cool clinical minimalism for DESIGN ATLAS — queue #5. Owned SSOT: InfoStyle `clinical`. Adapted for **web landing / app shell**. Not a medical claim system — visual calm + grid only.

## Traits

- Cool light ground `#F3F7F8`, ink `#24333A`
- Signals: steel blue `#4386A8`, teal `#58A69A`, soft blue `#718FC9`
- Strict grid feel, compact info blocks, even studio light
- Type: **Manrope** / DM Sans / Roboto — neutral, readable
- Distinct from soft-editorial (warm paper + Playfair) and Swiss (print red/black)

## Anti-patterns

- Invented medical approval / therapeutic claims in prompts
- Warm paper journal drift
- Neon / streetwear / dense industrial mosaics as hero
- Soft clay volume
