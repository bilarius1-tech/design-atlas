# Research notes · streetwear

## Intent

Bold streetwear / youth campaign system for DESIGN ATLAS — queue #1 after soft-volume + neon proofs. Owned SSOT: InfoStyle `market` (дерзкий streetwear). Adapted for **web landing / app shell**, not marketplace image-gen product cards.

## Traits

- Warm peach ground `#FFF3E8`, ink `#211C1A`
- Loud signals: coral `#FF5A36`, acid lime `#C7F000`, violet `#6246EA`
- Display **Unbounded** (dense, uppercase-friendly); Oswald / Roboto body
- Asymmetric energy: diagonal color planes, big type, short labels
- Distinct from `poster-brutal` (hard black offset / constructivist) — streetwear is warmer, multi-accent, campaign-loud

## Anti-patterns

- Soft clay / neu volume
- Frosted glass or neon dark-tech as the main idea
- Random stickers / fake logos
- Thin muted gray type on peach
