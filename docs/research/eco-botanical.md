# Research notes · eco-botanical

## Intent

Eco / botanical craft system for DESIGN ATLAS — queue #6. Owned SSOT: InfoStyle `eco`. Adapted for **web landing / app shell**. Visual nature language only — no invented product composition or eco-cert claims.

## Traits

- Warm paper / kraft ground `#EAE5D8`, ink `#34372C`
- Signals: leaf green `#73865B`, clay `#C47A4A`, honey `#D3A84B`
- Soft daylight feel; tactile, uncluttered
- Type: **DM Sans** / Manrope body; Playfair optional for display
- Distinct from soft-editorial (more fashion-journal) and clinical (cool blue)

## Anti-patterns

- Invented eco badges, ingredient claims, or “organic certified” seals in prompts
- Neon / streetwear / dense industrial mosaics
- Cold clinical blue-gray as hero
- Soft clay 3D volume as the main idea
