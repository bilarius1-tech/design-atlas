# Research notes · blueprint-grid

## Intent

Engineering blueprint / technical drawing system for DESIGN ATLAS — queue #2. Owned SSOT: InfoStyle `blueprint`. Adapted for **web landing / app shell**, not marketplace image-gen product cards.

## Traits

- Deep blue-graphite ground `#102631`, ink `#E8F5F5`
- Signals: cyan `#62D5FF`, amber `#FFB547`, lime `#A9E66E`
- Fine coordinate grid; dimension-line / contour energy
- Type: **Roboto Mono** (labels + often headings), Oswald / Manrope body
- Ordered composition, air, precision — not neon bloom, not streetwear loudness

## Anti-patterns

- Uncontrolled neon glow (`neon-tech`)
- Soft clay / neu / frosted glass
- Fake dimension numbers / invented specs in prompts
- Warm peach campaign planes (`streetwear`)
