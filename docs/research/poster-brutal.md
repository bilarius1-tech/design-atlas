# Research notes · poster-brutal

## Intent

Original poster/brutal marketing look for DESIGN ATLAS R2 compare — constructed from Atlas rules, not imported packs.

## Checked references (discipline, not copy)

- Contemporary web brutalism discourse (e.g. brutalistwebsites.com as cultural map)
- Poster/display traditions: oversized type, thick rules, hard geometry
- WCAG 2.2 contrast for orange-on-paper pairs

## Differentiation vs swiss-international

| Axis | Swiss | Poster Brutal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Border | 1px hairline | 3px hard rule |
| Shadow | none | hard offset |
| Type | Helvetica objectivity | Arial Black punch |
| Accent | Signal red | Signal orange |

## Ban

Do not paste neo-brutal packs from DesignPrompts or scrape collections. Names, copy, and tokens stay Atlas-original.
