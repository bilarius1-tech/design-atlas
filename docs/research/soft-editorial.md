# Research notes · soft-editorial

## Intent

Calm journal / soft editorial system for DESIGN ATLAS — queue #4. Owned SSOT: InfoStyle `catalog` (спокойный журнал). Adapted for **web landing / app shell**, not marketplace image-gen product cards.

## Traits

- Warm light ground `#F5F1E9`, ink `#302B27`
- Soft signals: terracotta `#B57956`, sage `#7D8C72`, slate `#7A8FA3`
- Wide margins, airy rhythm — not dense-tile
- Type: **Playfair Display** headings; Manrope / DM Sans body
- Distinct from Swiss (cooler, stricter) and warm-premium (dark stage)

## Anti-patterns

- Dense flush mosaics / industrial 0-gap
- Neon / streetwear loudness
- Fake fashion logos or sticker clutter
- Thin gray type on warm paper
