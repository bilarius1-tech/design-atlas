# Research notes · scandi-calm

## Intent

Scandinavian calm / warm-white home system for DESIGN ATLAS — queue #9. Owned SSOT: InfoStyle `scandi`. Adapted for **web landing / app shell**. Quiet geometry and daylight — not dense-tile, not fashion-loud editorial.

## Traits

- Warm white ground `#F1EEE7`, ink `#35342F`
- Soft naturals: clay `#B57956`, sage `#7D8C72`, slate `#7A8FA3`
- Simple geometric planes, soft daylight, generous air
- Type: **Manrope** primary; DM Sans / Playfair sparingly
- Distinct from `soft-editorial` (more journal/Playfair-led) and `eco-botanical` (kraft/leaf craft)

## Anti-patterns

- Dense flush industrial mosaics
- Neon / streetwear / signal yellow
- Over-decorated “hygge” clutter and fake wood textures as noise
- Thin gray type on warm white
